A few things to know.

What does Meownitor monitor?

Only your Mac setup and recent activity timing. It doesn’t use a camera or measure your posture. Input Monitoring permission is optional and helps distinguish external keyboards; keystroke contents are never saved or sent. Without it, display detection still works. Downloads connect to GitHub.

Which Macs and languages are supported?

The universal alpha supports Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 13 or later. MacBook gets posture reminders; iMac gets small break reminders. Other desktop Macs do not show reminders. This website is in English, Korean, and Japanese; the native app currently supports English and Korean.

How do I add another cat?

Open Settings in the Mac menu bar app, choose a cat, and download it. The app verifies its checksum before installation. Optional packs can be removed from Settings. Website ZIP downloads are the same assets, for manual use or development.

Where can I report a little hiccup?

Tell us what happened, your macOS version, and which Mac you use. A screenshot helps, too. Open a GitHub issue ↗
